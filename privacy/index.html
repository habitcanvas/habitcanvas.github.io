Habit Canvas Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

Habit Canvas is a habit tracker for iPhone made by Burak, an independent developer based in Türkiye (“we”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. In short: your habits stay on your device, there are no accounts, ads or analytics, and we never see your data.

Data stored on your device

Your habits, the days you mark as done, your canvas layout, links between habits, groups and reminder times are saved only in a database on your iPhone. They are never uploaded to us or to any server we operate. If you use iCloud or computer backups, this data may be included in your device backup, which is controlled by you and Apple.

Reminders

Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No notification content or schedule is sent to us. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

Purchases and subscriptions

Habit Canvas Premium is sold through Apple’s App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never receive your name, email address or payment details. To check whether you have an active subscription, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID generated on your device, your App Store purchase and transaction information, and basic technical details such as app version, device model, operating system and country/currency. This information is used only to validate purchases and unlock Premium. It is not linked to your habit data, which never leaves your device.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or collect your name, email or contacts.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share your information.

Deleting your data

Deleting a habit in the app removes it and its history from your device. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. To ask for purchase records held by RevenueCat to be deleted, contact us at the address below.

Children

Habit Canvas is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: habitcanvas.app@gmail.com

Habit Canvas 隐私政策

生效日期：2026年10月5日

Habit Canvas 是由居住在土耳其的独立开发者 Burak（“我们”）开发的 iPhone 习惯追踪应用。本政策说明你在使用本应用时信息的处理方式。简而言之：你的习惯数据只保存在你的设备上；没有账户、广告或分析工具，我们永远看不到你的数据。

保存在设备上的数据

你的习惯、标记完成的日期、画布布局、习惯之间的连接、分组和提醒时间只保存在你 iPhone 上的数据库中，不会上传给我们或我们运营的任何服务器。如果你使用 iCloud 或电脑备份，这些数据可能会包含在由你和 Apple 控制的设备备份中。

提醒

提醒通知在你的设备上本地安排，通知内容和时间不会发送给我们。你可以随时在 iOS「设置」中关闭通知。

购买与订阅

Habit Canvas Premium 通过 Apple App Store 销售，付款完全由 Apple 处理；我们不会收到你的姓名、电子邮件地址或付款信息。为确认你是否拥有有效订阅，本应用使用订阅服务 RevenueCat。RevenueCat 会收到在你设备上生成的匿名用户 ID、App Store 购买和交易信息，以及应用版本、设备型号、操作系统和国家/货币等基本技术信息。这些信息仅用于验证购买并解锁 Premium，不会与始终保留在你设备上的习惯数据关联。

我们不会做的事

  • 我们不要求创建账户，也不收集你的姓名、电子邮件或通讯录。
  • 我们不使用分析、广告或追踪工具，也不会在其他应用或网站上追踪你。
  • 我们不会出售或分享你的信息。

删除你的数据

在应用中删除某个习惯，会将其及其记录从你的设备上移除。删除本应用会从你的 iPhone 上移除其全部数据。你可以在 iOS「设置」›［你的姓名］›「订阅」中管理或取消订阅。如需删除 RevenueCat 保存的购买记录，请通过以下地址联系我们。
